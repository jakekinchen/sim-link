   retains the complete trace, first divergence, strict-v2 margins, and mirror
   MP4 under the pre-registered chunk-consumption variants.
3. **Dataset proof.** R0 must contain the exact T20.23 base once plus 64-119
   newly admitted strict-v2 scripted successes, training-only MEAN_STD
   statistics, and held-out evidence excluded from training/statistics.
4. **Metric-twin proof.** A real Robo Scan export must become a metric
   `WorkcellBundle`, then pass sim-link I5 deterministic compilation with
   pinned robot geometry, scale, calibration, uncertainty, and lineage. A
   declared or synthetic fixture is not a real metric twin.
5. **Physical-canary proof.** Only after a learned strict-v2 simulation result,
   a compiled metric twin, observable-evaluator qualification, and a fresh
   owner-present central permit may one low-speed policy canary run. It must
   produce proposed/issued/measured actions, a hardware-observable verdict,
   and a paired sim/real trace. Scripted, shadow-only, or fixture motion is not
   a learned-policy canary.
6. **Demo proof.** A tracked proof index must let another person understand
   and replay the verified parts without reading the full ledger. It must never
   upgrade an absent metric twin or canary into a completed claim.
